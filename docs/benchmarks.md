# Benchmarks: publicly reported Italian solar PPA price levels

Purpose: a cited comparison for the fair PPA range shown by the app. Rule: a figure is listed only with its date and a source URL that was opened. Nothing here is estimated.

Research date: 2026-11-11. The app's current range is computed on a **synthetic placeholder price series** and is not comparable to the table below. The comparison must be redone after the real-data run.

| Figure | What it is | Period | Reported on | Source (opened) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60.67 EUR/MWh (-3.7% quarter on quarter) | Italy solar PPA price, LevelTen Energy European PPA Price Index | Q4 2025 | 9 Feb 2026 | https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/02/09/solar-wind-ppa-prices-continue-to-fall-in-europe/ |

Caveats on the one figure above:

- It is a secondary report (pv magazine quoting LevelTen). The article does not define the percentile (the same article mentions "P25" only for the continental index), the contract length or the delivery structure. I did not open LevelTen's own Italy page; the Q2 2026 LevelTen post I tried returned HTTP 404 and the report itself is subscriber-only.
- The article does not say whether this is an asking-price index or settled deals, nor whether it is pay-as-produced or specific to the South. Treat it as an order-of-magnitude check, not as a target.
- The app range is a pay-as-produced price net of imbalance and margin; the index is not defined on the same basis.

Other pages opened that contain **no** Italy-specific solar level (so no figure is taken from them):

- Pexapark, monthly index coverage: Italy +2.7% in May 2026 (https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/06/26/european-ppa-prices-rebound-as-hybrids-grow-storage-slows/) and +4.3% in July 2026 (https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/24/european-ppa-prices-rise-2-3-in-july/). Only percentage changes; the only level quoted is the pan-European composite (about 45 EUR/MWh), not Italy and not solar.
- Pexapark Oct 2025 weekly coverage (https://www.pv-tech.org/lack-of-pricing-consensus-slows-down-european-ppa-deals-pexapark/): European average 46.37 EUR/MWh, no Italian level.
